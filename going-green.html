Sustainability

Going Green

We harmonise ecological, economic and social goals, so growth across South Africa leaves communities and landscapes better than we found them.

Our statement

As town planners we meet climate change and air pollution head on, planning balanced growth for urban and rural areas alike so every community shares in it. We cut carbon, back renewable energy and protect equitable access to resources, with responsible land use at the centre of resilient, thriving places.

Three commitments

Planned in, not bolted on.

Reduce carbon emissions

We design for lower energy demand and shorter travel, cutting the development footprint from the master plan onward.

Promote renewable energy

We site and orient developments to make solar and clean energy practical, not an afterthought.

Ensure equitable access

We plan so water, services and green space reach every household, not only the well-served.

We address today's needs while safeguarding the ability of future generations to meet theirs.
