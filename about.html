Who we are

Who We Are

A South African planning, development and built-environment firm built to carry a development from first land-use question to final brick, with the credentials to prove it.

Our purpose

Comprehensive planning, architecture and construction for sustainable community development.

Iyadi Planning Solutions (Pty) Ltd is a South African planning, development and built-environment firm founded in 2021. We work alongside every level of government, real estate developers, businesses and community organisations to create inclusive places that lift quality of life.

Guided by sustainability and social justice, our multidisciplinary network holds each project to a standard of efficiency and excellence, delivering work that serves communities today and protects their options tomorrow.

How we work

Our approach

One accountable team, assembled for your project.

Iyadi works through a multidisciplinary network of trusted built-environment specialists, allowing the right professional team to be assembled for each project. Planning, surveying, GIS, engineering, architecture, environmental and cost expertise are drawn together under one accountable point of contact.

This model keeps your development moving from land-use approval to construction completion without the handovers that stall projects, and ensures every discipline a project needs is matched by a qualified, registered professional.

Our disciplines

The professions behind every project.
