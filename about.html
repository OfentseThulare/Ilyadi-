Who we are

Who We Are

A South African planning, development and built-environment firm built to carry a development from first land-use question to final brick, with the credentials to prove it.

Our purpose

Comprehensive planning, architecture and construction for sustainable community development.

Iyadi Planning Solutions (Pty) Ltd is a South African town planning and architectural firm founded in 2021 by Mr Mongezi Shabangu. We work alongside every level of government, real estate developers, businesses and community organisations to create inclusive places that lift quality of life.

Guided by sustainability and social justice, our specialists hold each project to a standard of efficiency and excellence, delivering work that serves communities today and protects their options tomorrow.

Mongezi Shabangu

Founder spotlight

Mongezi Shabangu

A registered professional town planner, Mongezi began his career at Alfred Duma Local Municipality before founding Iyadi in 2021. He pairs deep knowledge of planning law with hands-on grounding in architecture, project management and construction, which is why complex, multi-discipline approvals do not faze him.
